# Patch notes

## 1.7.0.0

- Best price works with the add-on on when the unload target is a selling point you don't own (Aldi, Harbor, Train
  Station, a BGA you haven't bought...): AutoDrive's best price setting picks the best-paying seller in the folder.
- Unchanged for your own places: when the unload target is a production, BGA or silo your farm owns, best price stays
  off and Top It Off fills it (your carrots still go to your veggie factory, not the Roadside Stand).
- The unloading station nearest the unload target (within 60 m) decides. No station near the target counts as your
  own, so best price stays off.
- The log says once per target which way it decided: "best price: target '...' is own / a seller".
- Description: Refuel/Repair refuels first, then repairs (it said "the closer one first").
